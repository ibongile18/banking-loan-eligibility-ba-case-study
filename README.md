# Banking Loan Eligibility & Risk Scoring Engine — Technology BA Case Study

## 1. Executive Summary
This project simulates an end-to-end **Technology Business Analysis** initiative for a retail/private banking digital lending product. It bridges business strategy, mathematical risk modelling, and software engineering to automate loan pre-approval decisions while maintaining strict regulatory affordability compliance (aligned with South African National Credit Act principles).

## 2. Business Problem & Objectives
* **The Problem:** Manual loan application reviews create a 48–72 hour turnaround bottleneck, leading to high customer drop-off and inconsistent affordability assessments.
* **The Objective:** Design an automated pre-approval workflow that evaluates applicant Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratios, credit scores, and account behavior in real time—instantly approving low-risk applicants, declining ineligible ones, and routing borderline cases to credit analysts.
* **Target Impact:** Reduce standard decision turnaround time by **>80%** while keeping default risk within acceptable thresholds.
